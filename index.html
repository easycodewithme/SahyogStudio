Questions

Where is Sahyog Studio?

Our head office, Sahyog Photo Studio & Printing Press, is in Rakhiyal (map). Our new branch, Sahyog Recording Studio, is at Office 013, First Floor, Mahadev Arcade, opp. Patel Kathiyawadi Hotel, Atarsumba–Kapadvanj Highway, Atarsumba 387610 (map).

Can I get passport and ID photos?

Yes. Walk in to the studio for passport, visa and ID photos, or call ahead.

Do you shoot weddings and pre-wedding sessions?

Yes. We photograph and film weddings, engagements, haldi, mehndi and sangeet, and plan outdoor pre-wedding shoots.

Do you do balloon decoration for birthdays?

Yes. We set up balloon decoration for birthdays, baby showers and other family functions, and can photograph the event too.

How do I book a shoot?

Message us on WhatsApp or call the studio with your date and the kind of shoot you need.
