## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS (user choice). No build step; deployable to GitHub Pages / Vercel / Netlify.

## Users

People in and around Dahegam (Gandhinagar district, Gujarat) looking for a local photographer: families planning weddings and pre-wedding shoots, parents booking birthdays and baby showers, and walk-in customers needing passport/ID photos or portraits. Most arrive on a phone, often from a WhatsApp share, Instagram or a Justdial/Google listing, and want to see work and contact the studio quickly.

## Product Purpose

Portfolio and contact hub for Sahyog Studio (listed as "Sahyog Photo Studio", Justdial lists it under Dahegam). Success = a visitor sees the quality of the work and calls or WhatsApps the studio, or finds it on the map.

## Positioning

A local, full-service studio: wedding and pre-wedding photography, studio portraits and passport photos, event coverage plus balloon decoration, videography and album printing, all under one roof in Dahegam.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Sahyog Studio.
- Theme: black-and-white interface (user-pinned); photos stay in full colour (user request, 2026-10-26).
- Direction: the classic photography-portfolio canon, chosen on purpose over bolder worlds (2026-10-26). Quality bar: top Indian wedding studios (The Wedding Salad, House on the Clouds, Stories by Joseph Radhik): cinematic hero, large galleries, clear enquiry path.

## Product Principles

1. The work leads: photos before words.
2. One tap to contact: call and WhatsApp always reachable on mobile.
3. Local and honest: real address, real services, no invented claims.
4. Fast on cheap phones and patchy mobile data.
